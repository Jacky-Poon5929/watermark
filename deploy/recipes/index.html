01 / Prepare ahead

The butter chicken spice bag

Mix once, cook five times. Each curry uses 48 g of this universal blend with 1 kg chicken: 18 g for the marinade and 30 g for the sauce.

The quantities have been rounded slightly for easier weighing. This combined adaptation of the previous mixes means the marinade now contains a little sugar and sauce seasoning too.

One mix, two uses per curry. Use 18 g in the chicken marinade and 30 g in the curry sauce. The selector scales the dry mix only; the ingredients below are always for one curry.

Combine thoroughly and divide into 48 g portions, or keep in one airtight jar and stir or shake thoroughly before measuring each time. Sugar and salt can settle differently from the lighter spices. The standard 240 g batch makes five complete curries, using 5 kg chicken in total.

02 / For one curry

Butter chicken curry sauce

For one curry with 1 kg chicken. Tick off ingredients as you prepare.

No additional salt, sugar or onion powder is needed in the sauce: they’re already in your spice bag.
03 / For one curry

The chicken marinade

Mix thoroughly with the chicken and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, preferably 4 hours or overnight.

Use 18 g from the same universal spice mix used for the sauce. No separate marinade spice mix is needed.
04 / At the stove

Bring it all together

  1. Sear the chicken

    Around 3–4 minutes per side

    Heat a sauté pan over medium to medium-high heat with a little oil. Cook the marinated chicken in batches until lightly browned, then set aside. Because the universal spice mix contains sugar, watch for scorching.

  2. Prepare the sauce

    1–2 minutes for the paste

    Add approximately 25 g of the butter and 25 g ginger and garlic paste to the pan. Cook for 1–2 minutes. If using onion, soften it first.

  3. Add spice mix

    Stir in 30 g of your prepared spice mix and immediately add 875 g tomato passata to prevent the sugar and spices burning. Add the bay leaf if using.

  4. Simmer

    12–15 minutes

    Cook gently, stirring occasionally.

  5. Finish the curry

    Approximately 5–10 minutes, or until cooked through

    Add the chicken, 155 ml cream and remaining 50 g butter. Simmer until the chicken reaches at least 75°C internally. Add 1 tsp crushed kasuri methi if using, and remove the bay leaf.

A finishing touch. Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) adds a distinctive restaurant-style flavour. Keep it separate from the bulk blend and add it near the end of cooking.

Our adaptation of chef Vivek Singh’s Old Delhi-style butter chicken, designed for convenient batch preparation.
