    Choose the right format

    Use JPEG for photos, PNG for lossless images and transparency, or WebP for compact web files. BMP and TIFF are larger, uncompressed files. ICO fits your image inside a transparent 256 × 256 square.

    A few things to know

    *Some input formats depend on your browser, and some HEIC variants may not decode. Animated images, HEIC collections, and multi-page TIFFs become one still image. Metadata isn’t preserved. Output is 8-bit colour; HEIC/HEIF, GIF, SVG, and AVIF export aren’t available.
